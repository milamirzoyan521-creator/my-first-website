Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, how information is handled on this website. It is intentionally short.

About this website

Peak Fitness Yerevan is a fictional business. This site is a demo built as a web-design portfolio piece — it does not represent a real gym, and no real memberships, products, or services are offered or sold here.

What the contact form collects

The contact form is the only place this site asks you for personal information. When you submit it, it collects:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you write

These details are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service, which forwards your submission to the site owner's email inbox so your message can be read and replied to. Your submission is also subject to Formspree's own privacy policy. The membership sign-up popup works the same way, collecting only your name, email, and the plan you select.

What isn't collected

No other personal data is collected or stored by this site. There are no user accounts, no database, and no profile is built about you.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not run analytics — only what is needed for the page to function normally. (Formspree may process data or set its own cookies when you submit a form; see its policy for details.)

Questions

If you have any questions about privacy on this site, email milamirzoyan521@gmail.com.
