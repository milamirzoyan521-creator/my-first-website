01 — Weightlifting

Master The Barbell

Our weightlifting program is built around the big three — squat, bench, and deadlift — plus Olympic-style variations for lifters ready to go further. Every session starts with technique, because good numbers only last as long as good form.

  • —Small-group and 1:1 coaching on proper bar path and bracing
  • —Progressive programming that adds weight only when you're ready
  • —Access to competition-grade platforms, bars, and bumper plates
  • —Options for total beginners through competitive powerlifters

About Us

Built By Lifters, For Lifters

Peak Fitness Yerevan opened its doors with one goal: build a gym where beginners feel welcome and serious athletes feel challenged. Our coaches bring years of experience in strength training, competitive lifting, and group coaching — and every program we run is designed to meet you exactly where you are.

We believe fitness is a long game. That's why we focus on sustainable habits, proper form, and real community — not shortcuts. Come train with us and see the difference.
